Why Bagging & Boosting Excel

Single decision trees suffer from high variance on sparse text vocabularies. Ensemble bagging (Random Forest) and boosting (AdaBoost, XGBoost) neutralize overfitting by aggregating complementary decision paths.

Negation-Aware Preprocessing

Traditional bag-of-words ignores word order. EnsembleIQ's pipeline binds negation markers directly into compound tokens (e.g. not_expecting, not_good), preserving vital semantic reversal.

Sublinear TF Trigrams

Extracting unigrams through trigrams with sublinear term-frequency scaling allows phrases like "proved me wrong" and "complete waste of" to act as strong discriminative features.
